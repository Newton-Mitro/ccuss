## Purpose

A premium monthly deposit scheme designed to help members build long-term savings toward a large maturity value.

## Core policy

- Eligibility: Christian individual with a Dhaka Credit Savings account.
- Tenure: 5 to 20 years.
- Multiple accounts are permitted.
- One missed installment extends maturity by one month.
- Three consecutive missed installments cause closure and transfer of the net payable amount to the linked account.
- Premature withdrawal: interest is paid at the prevailing Savings rate; no interest is paid if withdrawal occurs before one year.
- Advance installments may be deposited, but no additional interest is paid for advance payment.
- Loan up to 95% of deposited amount after 1 year.
- Published table states an interest rate of 9.50% and contains tenure/initial-deposit/monthly-deposit schedules.
