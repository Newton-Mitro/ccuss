## Purpose

A recurring savings product intended to build regular saving habits for future financial needs.

## Core policy

- Monthly deposits: BDT 200, 300, 500, 1,000, and 1,500.
- From BDT 2,000 onward, deposits must be in multiples of BDT 1,000.
- Maximum monthly deposit stated: BDT 50,000.
- Interest: 8.5% for 5 years and 9% for 10 years.
- Tenures: 5 or 10 years.
- Loan is available after 2 years, up to 90% of total deposit.
- Any Savings account holder may open one or multiple accounts.
