## Purpose

A fixed-term deposit scheme intended to provide a doubled maturity benefit.

## Core policy

- Minimum deposit: BDT 10,000.
- Tenure: 6.5 years / 78 months.
- Loan up to 95% of deposited amount from account opening, subject to product conditions.
- A “Double loan” facility against the deposit is stated.
- Deposit can be used as surety for self/family member loans.
- Emergency premature withdrawal is allowed subject to conditions.

## Requirements

Individual: NID, nominee photo, membership/savings number; non-account-holder nominee requires NID/birth certificate and photo.
Joint: joint savings and individual savings account documentation plus nominee documentation.
Organization: board resolution, applicant NID/photo, authorization letter.
