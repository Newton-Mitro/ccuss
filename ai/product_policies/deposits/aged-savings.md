## Purpose

A long-term savings scheme designed for financial security around retirement.

## Core policy

- Monthly deposits: BDT 200, 300, 500, 700, 1,000, 1,500, and 2,000.
- Interest rate: 9% per annum.
- Tenures: 10, 15, 20, 25, or 30 years.
- After 2 years, up to 90% of the deposit amount can be received as a loan for emergencies.
- Premature withdrawal is allowed subject to conditions.
- Any Savings Account holder can open the account.
