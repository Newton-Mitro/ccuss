## Purpose

A share account designed to provide membership-related facilities and support self-reliance.

## Core policy

- Minimum monthly deposit: BDT 10.
- Minimum opening amount: BDT 330.
- Dividend is determined according to the Annual General Meeting (AGM).
- Members may provide loan surety for themselves and/or another member.
- Members are entitled to apply for various loans.

## Eligibility

The source states that Christian individuals aged 18 or above residing in Dhaka, Gazipur, Narayanganj, and Munshiganj are eligible.

## Requirements

- Two stamp-size and two passport-size color photographs.
- Baptism certificate and government birth certificate or NID.
- Individual applicants: NID, nominee photo and membership/savings details; non-account-holder nominee requires NID/birth certificate and photo.
- Utility bill.
- The source states that applicants in mixed marriages are not eligible.

Do not quote a fixed dividend percentage; the source says the dividend is decided by the AGM.
