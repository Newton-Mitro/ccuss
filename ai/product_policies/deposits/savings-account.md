## Purpose

A general savings product intended to provide a financial foundation and access to Dhaka Credit services.

## Core policy

- Minimum deposit: BDT 10 per month.
- Interest rate stated on the source page: 3% per annum.
- Interest is described as daily accrued and paid half-yearly.
- Full membership is stated to be available after 3 months of successful operations.
- Deposits and withdrawals are allowed at any time, subject to conditions.

## Eligibility and requirements

- Savings account is mandatory for opening any other type of account.
- The source states eligibility for Christian individuals of any age residing in Dhaka, Gazipur, Narayanganj, and Munshiganj.
- Individual documentation includes applicant NID, nominee photo and account/member details; non-account-holder nominees require NID/birth certificate and photo.
- Joint accounts require joint and individual savings account documentation.
- Organization accounts require board resolution, NID, applicant/nominee photos, authorization letter, and utility bill.
- The source states that applicants in mixed marriages are not eligible.
