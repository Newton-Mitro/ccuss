## Purpose

A member health-support scheme funded through regular monthly premiums.

## Core policy

- Duration: 5 years; renewable.
- Savings Account with Dhaka Credit is required.
- Savings holders under 18 may open through a guardian.
- Available up to age 70 at entry according to the source.
- Premiums: BDT 100, 200, 500, 1,000, 2,000, and 5,000.
- Applicants over 50 must take at least BDT 200 premium.
- Policy remains valid up to age 75 according to the source.
- Members above 50 are required to provide specified health certificates.
