## Purpose

A savings scheme intended to build funds for children's education in Bangladesh or abroad.

## Core policy

- Monthly deposits: BDT 200, 300, 500, 1,000, 2,000, or 3,000.
- Interest rate: 9% per annum.
- Tenures: 5, 10, 15, or 18 years.
- Loan available after 1 year, up to 80% of total deposit.
- After 5 years or completion of HSC, the source states that a loan up to twice the self-deposit may be received at 9% interest per annum.
- The source also mentions an instant loan facility for emergencies.
- One account per person is stated, for an age range from 1 month to 18 years.
