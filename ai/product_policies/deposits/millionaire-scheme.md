## Purpose

A premium monthly deposit scheme intended to accumulate BDT 1,000,000.

## Core policy

- Tenures: 3, 5, 8, 10, 12, and 15 years.
- Published monthly deposits to target BDT 1,000,000:
  - 3 years: BDT 24,300
  - 5 years: BDT 13,275
  - 8 years: BDT 7,175
  - 10 years: BDT 5,175
  - 12 years: BDT 3,890
  - 15 years: BDT 2,650
- After 3 years, up to 95% of total deposit can be taken as a loan.
- Deposit can be used as surety for self/family member loans.
- One missed monthly installment extends maturity by one month.
- Premature withdrawal is permitted subject to conditions.
- Savings account holders can open one or multiple accounts.
