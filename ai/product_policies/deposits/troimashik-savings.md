## Purpose

A fixed-deposit-type product that pays accrued interest quarterly to the holder's Savings/STD account.

## Core policy

- Minimum opening deposit: BDT 100,000.
- Amount above BDT 100,000 may be deposited.
- Interest rate: 10% per annum.
- Tenures: 4, 5, or 8 years.
- Loan up to 90% of deposit from account opening, subject to conditions.
- “Double loan” against deposit is stated.
- Deposit may be used as surety for self/family member loans.
- Interest is disbursed quarterly.
- Emergency premature withdrawal is allowed subject to conditions.
- Source examples indicate quarterly interest of 2.5% of principal (e.g. BDT 100,000 -> BDT 2,500 per quarter).
