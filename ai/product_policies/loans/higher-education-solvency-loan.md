## Purpose

A solvency facility for members pursuing higher education abroad.

## Core policy

- Maximum loan: BDT 1 crore.
- Interest: 12%.
- Tenure: 6 months.
- Interest amount must be deposited in full into Savings before receiving the loan.
- Applicant must satisfy student bank-solvency requirements.
- Applicant issues a cheque in favor of Dhaka Credit equal to the loan amount.

## Designated bank requirement

The source lists Standard Bank (Panthapath/Green Road), Citizens Bank PLC (Karwan Bazar Sub-Branch), BRAC Bank (Progoti Sarani), and IFIC Bank (Tejturi Bazar).

## Documents

Photo, NID/DC ID, passport, offer letter, educational certificates/marksheet, IELTS certificate, MICR cheque, and undertaking.
