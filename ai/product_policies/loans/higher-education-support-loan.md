## Purpose

Financial support for members pursuing higher education in Bangladesh or abroad.

## Core policy

- Maximum loan: BDT 1 crore.
- Interest: 12% yearly.
- Repayment starts 6 months after course completion.
- Maximum tenure: 5 years / 60 monthly installments.
- Eligible courses include undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma courses in government-affiliated universities.
- Loan amount is stated to be up to 20 times the member's share value.
- 100% collateral (book surety/mortgage) is required.

## Eligibility and documents

- Applicant must be a Dhaka Credit member.
- Minimum academic requirement: at least B grade / 2nd division in both SSC and HSC.
- Documents include photo, NID/DC ID, admission and tuition fee receipts, student ID, educational certificates/marksheets, and undertaking.
- Overseas education additionally requires offer letter, passport copy, and IELTS certificate.
