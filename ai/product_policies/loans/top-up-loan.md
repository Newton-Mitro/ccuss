## Purpose

Additional financing while a General Loan is still active.

## Core policy

- Interest: 12%, continuous reducing method; the source text describes this as “daily basis”.
- Applicant may apply after repaying 25% of the current General Loan.
- Additional amount depends on share, surety, income, and other capability.
- Running loan must have been active for at least one year.
- Current and previous loans must be regular.
- Existing General Loan must be adjusted from the granted loan.
- If any family member is a loan defaulter, the loan will not be approved.
- Application and documentation follow new-loan requirements.
