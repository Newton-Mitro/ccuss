## Purpose

A digital/urgent loan facility for members using Dhaka Credit MFS.

## Eligibility

- Old members: last 3 months' share deposits must be regular.
- New members: last 12 months' share deposits must be regular.
- Age: 25 to 65.
- All loans of the member and family members must be regular.
- Maximum two members from the same family may apply.
- Share balance must be at least 10% of requested loan.
- 25% of approved loan must be kept as lien in the member's savings account.
- Only members using Dhaka Credit MFS are eligible.

## Ceilings

1st: BDT 10,000.
2nd: BDT 20,000, available 3 months after first.
3rd: BDT 30,000, available 4 months after second.
4th: BDT 40,000, available 6 months after third.
5th: BDT 50,000, available 6 months after fourth.
Each ceiling must be repaid regularly. At least 50% of a ceiling must be received to qualify for the next ceiling. Default at a ceiling causes disqualification from the next ceiling and restart from the first ceiling.

## Pricing and repayment

Monthly interest: 1.25%. Maximum tenure: 180 days; loans up to BDT 10,000 must be repaid within 90 days. Minimum monthly payment is 5% of principal plus applicable interest. A service charge applies.
