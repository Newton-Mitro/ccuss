## Purpose

Financing for construction of a high-rise building in Dhaka.

## Core policy

- Maximum: BDT 3,00,00,000 (3 crore) or 70% of current land market value, whichever is lower.
- Interest: 13.5% yearly, continuous reducing method.
- Tenure: 30 years / 360 monthly installments.
- During construction, installments are not required, but interest must be paid.
- Borrower may repay from other sources.
- After construction, Dhaka Credit may assign a representative to rent flats and collect rent toward installments and interest.
- Source states: installment = Total Collected Rent − Interest when there is a disparity.

## Requirements

- Regular member.
- Age 25–60.
- At least one year bank statement.
- Financial capacity to construct quality foundation/grade beam.
- HDS requirement stated on source as BDT 200,000 Lac; this wording appears ambiguous and must be confirmed before operational use.
- Approved building plan and utility details.
- Updated land papers and income evidence.
- TIN certificate with return acknowledgement.
- Photo, NID, DC ID, family consent, MICR cheque.
- Irrevocable General Power of Attorney to Dhaka Credit.
