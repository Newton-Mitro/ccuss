## Purpose

A deposit-backed loan for members who have adequate Fixed Deposits but cannot provide conventional surety.

## Eligibility and core policy

- First loan must have been repaid through at least 6 months of regular installments.
- 5% of loan must be available in Share/Savings/HDS.
- May be applied for even with an existing General Loan or if the guarantor is in default.
- Loan is sanctioned as double against the member's Dhaka Credit deposit, up to BDT 40 lakh.
- After 3 months of FDR opening, member becomes eligible.
- Tenures: 36/60/72/96/120 installments.
- Interest: 12% yearly.
- Rebate is stated as available.
- Refinancing is available after repayment/settlement.

## Security and recovery

- Three MICR cheques in applicant's own name.
- Non-judicial stamp affidavit from an earning family member; source lists husband, wife, son, or daughter, with an alternative for applicants without an earning family member and a special rule for retired applicants.
- Interest and installments are calculated daily from disbursement.
- Default beyond 3 months may be reconciled with the deposit under deposit encashment policy, followed by recovery action under the applicable recovery policy.
- Board-approved changes/additions to the loan policy are subject to the Board's jurisdiction.
