## Purpose

Education financing for students and parents for higher education.

## Core policy

- Maximum loan: BDT 50,000.
- Interest: 12% yearly.
- Tenure: 3 years / 36 equal installments.
- Repayment begins 6 months after course completion.
- Source says priority is given to potential good Christian students for general education such as BA, Hons, MA, BBA, MBA, CA.

## Eligibility and security

- Student must achieve at least first division (A-) in one exam and second division (B) in other examinations including SSC and HSC.
- Membership minimum: 6 months.
- Share minimum: BDT 3,000.
- Security: 110% book surety, or 30% book surety + 70% MICR cheque.
- Documents include photo, NID/DC ID, admission receipt, tuition fee receipt, student ID, educational certificates/marksheets, and undertaking.
