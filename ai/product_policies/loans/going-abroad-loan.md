## Purpose

Financial assistance for members traveling abroad.

## Core policy

- Maximum loan: BDT 1 crore.
- Interest: 13.5%.
- Total loan interest must be deposited at once in Savings before receiving the loan.
- General tenure: 6 months.

## Requirements

- Regular Dhaka Credit member.
- Bank account with one of the designated banks listed by the source: Standard Bank (Panthapath/Green Road), Citizens Bank PLC (Karwan Bazar Sub-Branch), BRAC Bank (Progoti Sarani), or IFIC Bank (Tejturi Bazar).
- Member must satisfy bank solvency requirements.
- Cheque in favor of Dhaka Credit equal to loan amount.
- MICR cheque, recent photo, NID/DC ID, passport copy.
