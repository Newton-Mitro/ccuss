## Purpose

General financial support for productive and personal purposes for regular members.

## Core policy

- Maximum loan: BDT 40,00,000 (40 lakh).
- Interest: 12% yearly.
- Equal-installment tenures: 36, 60, 72, 96, 120, 144, or 180 installments depending on loan amount.
- 10% rebate on paid interest is stated for regular loanees.
- Loan Protection Scheme (LPS) coverage is available.
- Top-up and refinancing facilities are available for regular loanees.
- Surety structure: 30% book surety and 70% MICR cheque surety.
- General Savings and HDS deposits count as share for loan purposes.
