## Purpose

Financing to help members purchase a flat/apartment in Dhaka.

## Core policy

- Maximum loan: BDT 1,00,00,000 (1 crore).
- Interest: 13.5% yearly, continuous reducing method.
- Tenure: 30 years / 360 monthly installments.
- At registration, borrower provides power of attorney to Dhaka Credit.
- At registration, Dhaka Credit pays the loan directly to the builder/seller.

## Eligibility and requirements

- Regular Dhaka Credit member.
- Maximum age: 60.
- HDS: BDT 100,000 for loans around BDT 20 lakh; BDT 200,000 if above BDT 20 lakh.
- Sufficient surplus income.
- Flat purchase agreement and property papers.
- Income evidence and at least one year bank statement.
- TIN certificate with return acknowledgement.
- Recent photo, NID, DC ID, family consent letter, and MICR cheque.
