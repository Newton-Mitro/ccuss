## Purpose

Urgent medical financial support connected to pregnancy and childbirth / children aged 0–18 months.

## Core policy

- Maximum loan: BDT 200,000.
- Interest: 12% yearly, continuous reducing method.
- Tenure: 36 equal monthly installments.
- Processing fee: BDT 500 with loan application.
- Fines for missed installments follow General Loan rules.
- LPS is similar to General Loan.
- No interest rebate.
- Existing General Loan does not prevent application.
- Savings and Credit Account must be maintained for at least 3 months.

## Requirements

- Dhaka Credit digital ID copy, loan application, recent photo.
- Child age must be 0–18 months.
- Health Care Scheme Account required.
- Three signed blank MICR cheques before receiving the loan.
- Savings and Share accounts must be deposited regularly.
